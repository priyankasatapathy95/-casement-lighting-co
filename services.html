Services

Lighting design services

Every project starts with a consultation — from there, we scope one of the services below (or a mix) around your space and budget.

Residential Lighting Design

A full lighting plan for a room, floor, or whole home — layered so each space works for reading, cooking, hosting, and winding down.

  • Room-by-room lighting plan
  • Fixture and bulb temperature recommendations
  • Dimming and control layout

Commercial & Retail Lighting

Storefront and interior lighting that draws attention to merchandise and guides how customers move through the space.

  • Storefront and window lighting
  • Accent lighting for displays
  • Energy-efficient fixture specification

Landscape & Exterior Lighting

Path, garden, and facade lighting designed for a warm evening feel without spilling glare onto neighboring properties.

  • Path and garden fixture layout
  • Facade and architectural uplighting
  • Low-voltage and solar options

Lighting Audits & Efficiency

A walkthrough of your current lighting with recommendations to cut energy use without losing the mood of a space.

  • Room-by-room lighting audit
  • LED retrofit recommendations
  • Estimated energy savings summary

Smart Lighting Integration

Scenes, schedules, and app or voice control layered on top of your lighting plan — set up so the defaults already feel right.

  • Smart bulb and switch setup
  • Scene and schedule configuration
  • Walkthrough for household members
