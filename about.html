About us

Lighting is the last thing people notice — and the first thing they feel.

Casement Lighting Co. was started to treat lighting as its own design discipline, not an afterthought once the walls are painted.

Our story

Why we started Casement

Casement Lighting Co. began as a small residential lighting consultancy after years of seeing the same pattern: a beautifully designed room, finished off with whatever ceiling fixture the builder had on hand. We believed lighting deserved the same intention as the furniture and paint choices around it.

Today we work with homeowners, retailers, and landscape architects to plan lighting from the earliest stage of a project — or to rework a space that never quite felt right after dark.

What guides us

Our values

Light for how you live

We start with how a room is actually used at different times of day, not a catalog of fixtures.

Layered, not flat

Ambient, task, and accent lighting are planned together so no space depends on a single switch.

Considerate by design

Exterior and landscape work is designed to avoid glare and light spill onto neighboring properties.

Built to last

We specify fixtures and controls we're confident will still work well in ten years, not just on install day.

How we work

Our process

Consultation

We walk the space (or review your floor plans) and talk through how each room is used, day and night.

Lighting plan

We put together a layered plan — ambient, task, and accent — with fixture and placement recommendations.

Sourcing & install

We coordinate fixtures and work with your electrician (or ours) to bring the plan to life.

Follow-up visit

A few weeks after install, we return to fine-tune dimming levels and angles once you've lived with it.
